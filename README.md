# rpg

A silly little RPG. Walk around and talk to NPCs, find the shop and crack the code to get a special card, battle Rebeka and Flicker, and solve some box puzzles!

## Controls

- WASD to move
- Q to interact and grab boxes
- Q to advance dialogue

Doors may be blocked by planks. Interacting won't help; you have to trigger the associated buttons or force them open with a different approach.

## Combat

- The cards you play are limited by the energy each card costs and by your playing slots.
- Jokers and Diamond cards will be equipped when played, giving you a permanent effect (for the duration of the battle).
- Block (blue bar) only lasts a single turn, so don't use your blocking cards up early!
- Block also only works for immediate normal damage. Beware of Fireballs and Sprangler's teeth!
- The little icons under your health bar let you perform a basic attack or block, if you don't select any cards to play that turn. However, you won't get very far with just these...
- You can improve your attacks and blocks permanently with a Diamond card like the Slick Sword or the Sturdy Shield (we're very good at names). This will also improve cards that use a basic attack or block. Cards like Barbaric Strike which always deal the same amount of damage will not benefit from this.
- To prevent soft-locking, whenever a battle ends, the player is healed by 5HP.
